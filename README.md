# Chalculator: How To Play
Players see the numbers, operators, and the answer to the equation and have to guess the original equation. Numbers can be combined to form larger numbers; for example 2 and 4 can be combined to make 24 and 42. 
<br>
<br>
An important thing to keep in mind is that you must use all numbers and operators that is displayed on the screen and that order of operations does not take into account, for example 2+4*3 will be calculated as 18 even though it's technically 14. It will always be left-to-right.
<br>
<br>
A four-function calculator is recommended to play this game, especially for questions with complex decimal answers.

# Current Features
<ul>
  <li>4 Operators (+, -, *, /)</li>
  <li>3 Difficulty Options (Easy, Medium, Hard)</li>
  <li>Detailed Game Introduction, Printed When the Game Loads</li>
  <li>Error Bound to Accept "Virtually the Same" Answers (currently +/- 0.001)</li>
  <li>6 Decimal Places to Prevent Answers from Occupying the Entire Screen</li>
</ul>
